# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good intent,
welcome different levels of experience, and focus technical discussion on ideas and observable
behavior.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation,
publication of another person's private information, and sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove
content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the
community.

Enforcement decisions should protect reporters' privacy, be proportionate, and avoid retaliation.
